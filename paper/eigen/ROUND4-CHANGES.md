# ROUND4-CHANGES: Paper B ("Finitely many eigenvalues determine the signature of a hyperbolic orbifold")

Response to referee round 4 (`review/referee-round-4/VERDICT-B.md` and the reports B-a to B-e). Item IDs
are those of VERDICT-B; numbers refer to the revised manuscript (25 pp.).

## What changed in the numbers (theory/eigen, round 4)

The computations behind Section 7 were redone (`theory/eigen/instances.py`, `theory/eigen/practice.py`,
`theory/eigen/METHODS.md`, `theory/eigen/practice.md`):

- **Systole lower bounds from a complete enumeration**: all group elements displacing a base point by at
  most L + 2r, L = 4 (every closed geodesic of length <= L has such a representative), at 40 and 50 digits
  in floating point (agreeing to 1e-30; not interval arithmetic); the value is rounded down at six decimals,
  a margin of at least 5e-8. O(2,8,8): 2.256767 (the old 2.256768 was not a lower
  bound); O(3,3,12): 1.862604; family 4b(ϑ).
- **Diameter bounds are proved**: 2 diam P for the double of a convex polygon P; the computed diameters equal
  diam P (2.448, 2.158; family 2.29-3.88).
- **Criteria and time grid**: both criteria of Theorem 7.1, (C1) and (C2), are implemented exactly; the
  coarse 17-point time grid is replaced by 700 log-spaced points refined around each minimiser, and the
  infimum over s by a 240-point grid confirmed by Brent's method. The old 21 and 39 for the triangle
  orbifolds were coarse-grid (C2)-type counts; now (C1) 18 and 25, (C2) 20 and 29 (referee d found 17-21
  and 25-35, consistent).
- **Full spectra**: the family uses the about 5800 computed eigenvalues per member, cut at 0.8 × the least
  sector maximum (4357-4451), not the first 120 per sector (815-856).
- **The member of systole 0.694 now passes**: with the full spectra, (C1) holds with 767 (M = 3) and 847
  (M = 12) eigenvalues, at t ≈ 0.004. So the test's criterion holds for **all ten** examples, and the
  headline says so. (On the old 120-per-sector lists, (C2) indeed failed for that member, which was the
  "nine of ten" of the brief; with the theorem's own criterion (C1) or with the full spectra all ten pass.
  We state what is true for the data now used.)

## Shared computed spectra (with Paper A, item n26 of VERDICT-A)

Both papers now describe the same computations: about 2850 eigenvalues per triangle orbifold and about
5800 per family member were computed (Paper A, supplement S4 and S6); Paper B uses only those in the ranges
where completeness is checked, about 2000 (λ < 1.6×10⁴) and 4357-4451 (below 0.8 × the least sector
maximum), and says so in §1 and §7.1.

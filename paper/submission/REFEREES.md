# Suggested referees (for the authors' choice)

Compiled 2026-10-10 from fetched public sources: institutional pages, the affiliations printed on recent papers (Crossref, arXiv), and the OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and ORCID public APIs. The source of every affiliation is given. An institutional email is given only where an institutional page prints it in plain text. Where a page shows the address in obfuscated form, the entry gives the page instead. No private contact details were collected.

**Conflict rules applied to every name below:**
- not a co-author of any of the six authors;
- not at an author institution: Indus International School, Texas A&M University, University of California Irvine, Lawrence High School (Lawrenceville), or DriveChange Learning and Resource Centre;
- not thanked in the papers (none of the three thanks a person);
- active, with a paper or preprint from 2024 or later.

**How the co-author check was done:**
- The authors' records were checked through their six ORCID iDs (ORCID and OpenAlex) and by name on Semantic Scholar.
- Each candidate's complete OpenAlex co-author list was searched for the authors' names. There were no hits.
- **Limit:** five of the six authors have no indexed publications, and "Jerry Wang" is too common a name to resolve. The authors should therefore confirm that they have no personal connection (teacher, mentor, programme) to anyone they choose.

Papers A and B may go to the same editor, so do not suggest the same person for both. Strohmaier and Dryden appear on both lists for that reason: as a main candidate on one list and as a reserve on the other.

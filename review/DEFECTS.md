## Severity scale

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FATAL | A false or unsupported claim in the manuscript on which a stated result, the abstract, or an availability statement rests |
| MAJOR | A referee will demand it: a missing proof, citation, definition or reconciliation |
| MINOR | Precision: a statement that needs narrowing, a citation to bring current, a research-record gap |
| COPY | Copyedit and submission hygiene |

summary: 'Dryden-Strohmaier, Canad. Math. Bull. 52 (2009) 66-71, DOI 10.4153/CMB-2009-008-0
  (matches manuscript citation exactly); arXiv:math/0504571 (6pp). Full-text-verified
  THEOREM 1.1 (verbatim): ''Let O be a compact orientable hyperbolic orbisurface.
  The Laplace spectrum of O determines its length spectrum and the number of cone
  points of each possible order. Knowledge of the length spectrum and the number of
  cone points of each order determines the Laplace spectrum.'' This is EXACT determinacy
  (no ''up to finitely many possibilities'' hedge, unlike Dryden''s earlier math/0411290)
  -- proved via the wave-trace singular-support method applied to the Selberg trace
  formula for orbisurfaces, exploiting linear independence of the functions psi_m(r)
  for distinct cone orders m. Paper notes P. Doyle and J.P. Rossetti independently
  proved the same result (arXiv:math/0605765). COROLLARY (Proposition 3.3, stated
  in paper): isospectral compact orientable hyperbolic orbisurfaces have the same
  underlying topological space (genus). NOT stated in the paper but a DIRECT LOGICAL
  CONSEQUENCE of Theorem 1.1 plus the elementary rigidity of hyperbolic triangle orbifolds
  (a (p,q,r) triangle orbifold has a unique hyperbolic structure up to isometry once
  p,q,r are fixed -- no continuous moduli): two Laplace-isospectral compact orientable
  hyperbolic triangle orbifolds must have the same cone-order multiset {p,q,r} by
  Theorem 1.1, hence must be ISOMETRIC. This appears to rule out non-isometric isospectral
  pairs among hyperbolic triangle orbifolds, though the corollary is not explicitly
  stated as such anywhere found in the literature (see VERDICT note).'

Abstract:
We show that for compact orientable hyperbolic orbisurfaces, the Laplace spectrum determines the length spectrum as well as the number of singular points of a given order. The converse also holds, giving a full generalization of Huber's theorem to the setting of compact orientable hyperbolic orbisurfaces.

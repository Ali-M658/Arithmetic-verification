summary: 'Bloom and Elsholtz, ''Egyptian Fractions'' (arXiv:2210.04496), a 10-page
  survey written for Nieuw Archief voor Wiskunde covering: the Erdos-Straus conjecture
  4/n=1/x+1/y+1/z and its generalizations; counting representations of a fixed rational
  as a sum of k unit fractions (the Elsholtz-Tao / Elsholtz-Planitzer / Browning-Elsholtz
  asymptotic-counting program); the Erdos-Graham density conjecture on unit-fraction
  representations of 1 from positive-density sets (resolved by Bloom, arXiv:2112.03726);
  and related open problems. As the most current comprehensive survey of the Egyptian-fraction
  research literature (2022), it is strong negative evidence for novelty: no section
  of this survey mentions or alludes to a problem of matching BOTH the integer sum
  AND the reciprocal sum across two distinct triples -- the survey''s organizing categories
  (single-representation counting, density/covering questions, restricted-denominator
  questions) do not include a ''two representations sharing two symmetric-function
  values'' category.'

Abstract:
Any rational number can be written as the sum of distinct unit fractions. In this survey paper we review some of the many interesting questions concerning such 'Egyptian fraction' decompositions, and recent progress concerning them.
Comments:
10 pages, survey article written for Nieuw Archief voor Wiskunde. Comments welcome!

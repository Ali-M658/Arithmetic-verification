summary: 'Aubel & Bolcskei, ''Vandermonde Matrices with Nodes in the Unit Disk and
  the Large Sieve,'' Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis (accepted), arXiv:1701.02538v3,
  DOI 10.48550/arXiv.1701.02538. Full PDF read (13 pages). Section 3.1 recaps Gautschi-Inglese''s
  exact lower bounds: kappa(V_{K x K}, ||.||_inf) >= (K-1)2^K for real positive nodes
  and >= 2^{K/2} for symmetric real nodes (their Thms 2.2, 3.1), and Beckermann''s
  sharper two-sided bound for real nonzero nodes: sqrt(2)(1+sqrt(2))^{K-1}/sqrt(K+1)
  <= kappa(V_{K x K}) <= (K+1)sqrt(2)(1+sqrt(2))^{K-1}, tightening for positive nodes
  to C_K/(2(K+1)) <= kappa <= (K+1)/2 * C_K with C_K = (1+sqrt2)^{2K}+(1+sqrt2)^{-2K}.
  Section 4 recounts the Selberg-Moitra connection between Vandermonde extremal singular
  values on the unit circle and the large-sieve inequality: kappa(V_{NxK})^2 <= (N-1+1/delta)/(N-1-1/delta)
  (Moitra, eq. 12) where delta is the minimum wrap-around separation of node frequencies
  -- i.e. condition number blows up as an explicit function of 1/(minimum separation).
  The paper''s own main result (Section 5) extends this to nodes in the unit disk
  via a generalized Hilbert inequality (their Theorem 2, eq. 35-36) with bound ~ e^{2*lambda/delta}/(delta(e^{2lambda/delta}-1))
  that again diverges as the minimum node separation delta -> 0. This paper is the
  single clearest confirmation that (i) the Vandermonde matrix IS the Jacobian of
  the power-sum map and (ii) its condition number blow-up is governed precisely by
  pairwise node separation, mirroring the manuscript''s blow-up on p=q, p=r, q=r.'

Title:
Vandermonde Matrices with Nodes in the Unit Disk and the Large Sieve
Authors:
Céline Aubel
,
Helmut Bölcskei
View a PDF of the paper titled Vandermonde Matrices with Nodes in the Unit Disk and the Large Sieve, by C\'eline Aubel and Helmut B\"olcskei
View PDF
Abstract:
We derive bounds on the extremal singular values and the condition number of NxK, with N>=K, Vandermonde matrices with nodes in the unit disk. The mathematical techniques we develop to prove our main results are inspired by a link---first established by by Selberg [1] and later extended by Moitra [2]---between the extremal singular values of Vandermonde matrices with nodes on the unit circle and large sieve inequalities. Our main conceptual contribution lies in establishing a connection between the extremal singular values of Vandermonde matrices with nodes in the unit disk and a novel large sieve inequality involving polynomials in z \in C with |z|<=1. Compared to Bazán's upper bound on the condition number [3], which, to the best of our knowledge, constitutes the only analytical result---available in the literature---on the condition number of Vandermonde matrices with nodes in the unit disk, our bound not only takes a much simpler form, but is also sharper for certain node configurations. Moreover, the bound we obtain can be evaluated consistently in a numerically stable fashion, whereas the evaluation of Bazán's bound requires the solution of a linear system of equations which has the same condition number as the Vandermonde matrix under consideration and can therefore lead to numerical instability in practice. As a byproduct, our result---when particularized to the case of nodes on the unit circle---slightly improves upon the Selberg-Moitra bound.

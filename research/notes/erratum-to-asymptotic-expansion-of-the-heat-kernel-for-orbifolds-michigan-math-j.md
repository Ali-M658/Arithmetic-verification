Michigan Math. J. 66 (2017), 221–222
Erratum to “Asymptotic Expansion of the Heat Kernel
for Orbifolds”
Emily B. Dryden, Carolyn S. Gordon,
Sarah J. Greenwald, & David L. Webb
In [1], for each stratum N of the singular set, we deﬁne a subgroup Isomax(N)
of the isotropy group of N. The subgroups Isomax(N) play an important role
in the heat invariants. In Theorem 5.1 (one of the applications of the heat in-
variants), there is an implicit assumption that Isomax(N) is nontrivial. Thanks
to a question from Naveed Bari, we now realize that Isomax(N) may be triv-
ial. The strata for which Isomax(N) is trivial do not appear in the heat invari-
ants, necessitating the addition of a hypothesis to Theorem 5.1. An example
for which Isomax(N) is trivial and the modiﬁed statement of Theorem 5.1 fol-
low.

The quotient of R3 by G is an orbifold. The axes project to 1-dimensional strata,
and the origin projects to a 0-dimensional stratum for which Isomax(N) is trivial.
5.1. Theorem. Let O be a Riemannian orbifold with singularities. If O is even di-
mensional (respectively, odd dimensional) and if there exists an odd-dimensional
(respectively, even-dimensional) O-stratum N of the singular set with Isomax(N)
nontrivial, then O cannot be isospectral to a Riemannian manifold.
We remark that Isomax(N) is nontrivial for all strata N that have maximal dimen-
sion within any given component of the singular set.

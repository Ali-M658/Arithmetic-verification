  is ''the first'' non-zero half-power heat coefficient detected for conic singularities
  (no half powers occur for smooth closed surfaces or for the flat-cone case treated
  in the prequel arXiv:1701.01874). *** CRITICAL DOWNSTREAM FINDING (from reading
  Schueth arXiv:2511.22255 Remark 3.3, cross-checked in the vault): Schueth''s 2025/2026
  paper explicitly states her formula for b_{1/2}(C) ''differs fundamentally from
  the one which is stated in [this paper]'', identifies the discrepancy source as
  the pole of Gamma(-s/2) at s=0 having ''seems to have been ignored in the computation
  starting in the lower part of p. 9'' of this Suleymanova paper, and additionally
  flags a smaller sign slip (cot(phi) written instead of 1/sin(phi) on p.10) that
  is ''not the main reason'' for the difference. Schueth''s corrected b_{1/2}(C) is
  IRRATIONAL in f''(0) (via a nontrivial special-function integral), contradicting
  this paper''s claim that b_{1/2} scales rationally (linearly) under constant rescaling

  b_{1/2} for curved conic surface singularities, but per Schueth 2025/2026 its stated

  upon for exact coefficients; Schueth''s arXiv:2511.22255 supersedes it.'

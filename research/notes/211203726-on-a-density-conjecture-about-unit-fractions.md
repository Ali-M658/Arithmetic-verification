  appendix co-written by Bhavik Mehta). Proves: any set A of positive integers with
  positive upper density contains a FINITE subset S subset A with sum_{n in S} 1/n
  = 1, answering a question of Erdos and Graham. This is the ''equal sums of unit
  fractions with no sum-of-denominators condition'' branch of the literature (task''s
  category (c)): it is about representing the single fixed value 1 (not matching two
  independent triples on two symmetric functions), and about a density/covering existence
  question rather than an exact counting or pairing question. Confirms no structural
  connection to Section 5''s equal-sum-and-equal-reciprocal-sum pairing problem.'

Title:
On a density conjecture about unit fractions
Authors:
Thomas F. Bloom
View a PDF of the paper titled On a density conjecture about unit fractions, by Thomas F. Bloom
View PDF
Abstract:
We prove that any set $A\subset \mathbb{N}$ of positive upper density contains a finite $S\subset A$ such that $\sum_{n\in S}\frac{1}{n}=1$, answering a question of Erdős and Graham.

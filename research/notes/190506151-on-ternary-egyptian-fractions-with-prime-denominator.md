summary: 'Luca and Pappalardi, ''On ternary Egyptian fractions with prime denominator''
  (arXiv:1905.06151, published Res. Number Theory, DOI 10.1007/s40993-019-0172-z).
  Defines A_k(n) = number of positive integers a such that a/n = 1/m1+1/m2+...+1/mk
  has a solution in positive integers m_i, and proves the asymptotic bound x(log x)^3
  << sum_{p<=x} A_3(p) << x(log x)^5 as x -> infinity, summed over primes p. This
  is a direct-count (not equal-sum-constrained) asymptotic result for THREE-term unit-fraction
  representability, in the spirit of but distinct from Elsholtz-Tao''s f(p) results
  for the fixed-numerator-4 Erdos-Straus case: here the numerator a is allowed to
  vary over all representable residues mod n=p, and the paper bounds the total count
  A_3(p) rather than a single f(p).'

On ternary Egyptian fractions with prime denominator

Abstract:
Given a positive integer $n$ we let $A_k(n)$ be the number of positive integers $a$ such that $\frac{a}{n}=\frac{1}{m_1}+\frac{1}{m_2}+\cdots+\frac{1}{m_k}$ for some $m_1,m_2,\ldots,m_k\in {\mathbb N}$. We show that $x(\log x)^3\ll \sum_{p\le x} A_3(p)\ll x(\log x)^5$ as $x\rightarrow\infty$.

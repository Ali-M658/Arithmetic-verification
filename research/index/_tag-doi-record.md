THEOREM 1 (verbatim, load-bearing): for a curvilinear polygon Omega with corners of interior angle alpha_j and one-sided limiting boundary curvatures kappa_{j,+}, kappa_{j,-}, the Dirichlet heat trace is H^D_Omega(t) = |Omega|/(4 pi t) - |dOmega|/(8 sqrt(pi t)) + (1/12pi)(int_{dOmega} kappa ds + sum_j (pi^2-alpha_j^2)/(2 alpha_j)) + sqrt(t)[(1/(256 sqrt pi)) int_{dOmega} kappa^2 ds + sum_j C_{1/2}(alpha_j,kappa_{j,+},kappa_{j,-})] + O(t log t). THEOREM 2 gives an explicit sector-heat-kernel-integral formula for the angle factor c_{1/2}(alpha), and the paper further proves the Dirichlet corner contribution has the closed form C_{1/2}(alpha,kappa_+,kappa_-) = c_{1/2}(alpha)(kappa_++kappa_-)/(4 sin(alpha/2)). This t^{1/2} term is the FIRST place boundary curvature and corner angle genuinely interact (it vanishes identically for exact sectors, kappa_+=kappa_-=0, and is absent from smooth-boundary local coefficients). SIGN LAW: c_{1/2}(alpha,kappa_+,kappa_-) sign is fixed by convex (alpha<pi, kappa_++kappa_->=0) vs reflex (alpha>pi, kappa_++kappa_-<=0) corners, giving a spectral obstruction: every convex curvilinear polygon Dirichlet-isospectral to a straight-sided polygon must itself be straight-sided (Corollary), sharpening Enciso-Gomez-Serrano's 'Spectral determination of semi-regular polygons' (J. Diff. Geom. 122(3), 399-419, 2022) by removing its straight-corners hypothesis. Cites Nursultanov-Rowlett-Sher (ref [11]) as 'Ann. Math. Quebec 49(1), pp.1-61 (2025)' for the exact-sector/straight-corner baseline case (no C_{1/2} sum needed there).

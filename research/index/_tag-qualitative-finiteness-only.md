Result: a Laplace-isospectral family of 2-dimensional Riemannian orbifolds sharing a lower Ricci/sectional-curvature bound contains only finitely many orbifold-category diffeomorphism types; more generally any collection of 2-orbifolds with lower curvature bound, lower volume bound, and upper diameter bound has finitely many diffeomorphism types. - [[11062724-orbifold-homeomorphism-finiteness-based-on-geometric-constraints]] — [1106.2724] Orbifold homeomorphism finiteness based on geometric constraints — Proctor (Emily), arXiv:1106.2724v1, published Ann. Glob. Anal. Geom., DOI 10.1007/s10455-011-9270-4 (Related DOI on arXiv page; not independently confirmed against publisher record here). Generalizes Proctor-Stanhope (arXiv:0811.0797) from 2-orbifolds to n-dimensional orbifolds with only isolated singular points: any collection of n-orbifolds with sectional curvature and volume bounded below, diameter bounded above, and isolated singularities contains only finitely many orbifold homeomorphism types (extending Grove-Petersen-Wu's manifold finiteness theorem to orbifolds); consequently any Laplace-isospectral collection of such orbifolds (curvature bounded below) is also finite up to orbifold homeomorphism type. METHOD (relevant to Q4/Q5): pure compactness argument -- every sequence has a subsequence converging to an orbifold limit, and Perelman's stability theorem supplies a homeomorphism between the limit and members of the tail of the sequence, which is then shown to respect orbifold structure. This is STILL a qualitative finiteness/compactness statement: no explicit numerical bound on the count of homeomorphism types is given as a function of the curvature/volume/diameter constants, confirming that even the natural generalization of Proctor-Stanhope remains effective-bound-free. `evergreen`

Main theorem: for a compact Lie group acting isometrically on a non-collapsing sequence of compact Alexandrov spaces with fixed dimension, uniform lower curvature bound, and upper diameter bound, if the action sequence is equicontinuous and converges equivariantly (equivariant Gromov-Hausdorff), the limit space is equivariantly homeomorphic to spaces in the tail of the sequence. COROLLARY (orbifold finiteness): the class of n-dimensional Riemannian orbifolds with a lower sectional-curvature bound, a lower volume bound, and an upper diameter bound has only finitely many members up to orbifold homeomorphism; any isospectral class of such orbifolds with a lower curvature bound is likewise finite up to orbifold homeomorphism -- generalizing Proctor-Stanhope's Theorem via equivariant Alexandrov-geometry convergence rather than smooth Riemannian convergence.

Traces the conjecture to Oblath (1948/1950), records Mordell's (1969) and Terzi's (1971) modular exception classes, Vaughan's (1970) bound x exp(-c(log x)^{2/3}) on exceptions up to x, verification up to n<=10^18 (Miller-Dumas, 2025), an equivalence to a covering-system-type statement (Bloom-Elsholtz 2022), and states the Elsholtz-Tao (2013) result sum_{p<=N} f(p) = N(log N)^{2+o(1)}, f(p)<=p^{3/5+o(1)}, and the Elsholtz-Planitzer (2020) almost-all lower bound f(n) >= (log n)^{log 6 + o(1)}. For f(n) = number of solutions (x,y,z) to 4/n = 1/x+1/y+1/z, proves N log^2 N << sum_{p<=N} f(p) << N log^2 N log log N (sum over primes p<=N), hence f(p) = O(log^3 p log log p) for a density-1 subset of primes, and the pointwise bound f(p) << p^{3/5} + O(1/log log p) for ALL primes p.
